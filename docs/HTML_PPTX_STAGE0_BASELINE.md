The historical F2 aggregate >=0.90 threshold passes all three. The stricter per-slide >=0.90 target in this delivery plan fails on `launch` slides 1 and 5. The earlier 2026-10-22 results (`.9137/.9475/.9573`) and `training` playback record are useful history, but are not substituted for this run. The latest OOXML inventory has source-specific effects, delays, durations, triggers, shape/paragraph targets and transitions in the manifest. It proves package representation and the fidelity model's animation score; it does not prove playback. **No fresh WPS slideshow playback was run in stage 0**. Stage 2 must observe it before animation acceptance.

Review of the comparison images finds that slide 1 has smaller WPS text and different pitch than the browser source. Slide 5 additionally omits the source's visible video player: `teaser.mp4` is absent from the fixture and the converter explicitly warns that unavailable media was omitted. This is a visible, reproducible cause of the slide-5 shortfall, but it does not establish whether a supported placeholder or media source can safely satisfy the stricter target. Stage 1 must inspect the two failing slides without adding external media, changing fixture content or weakening the warning. The remaining pages are regression controls. The source also contains SVG, script and hover behavior that the documented converter omits with warnings; a 1.0 semantic score therefore does not mean pixel equivalence or full web behavior.

The new script passes Ruff check and format with cached Ruff `0.16.8`; CI remains a separate gate.
