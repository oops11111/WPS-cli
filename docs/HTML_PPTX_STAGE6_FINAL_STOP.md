## Gates that prevent full acceptance

The new [launch slide 5 comparison](../artifacts/html-pptx-stage6-final-20261122/A-launch-pptx/pages/page-005-0.948.png) retains a source video control with no target object. `teaser.mp4` is absent from the source folder, `mapped_objects.media` is zero, and the converter warns that the video was omitted. This visible-object gate **fails** despite slide SSIM .9475.

The same source slide has `<span class="fade">全渠道开售</span>` inside a paragraph. The fresh slide 5 OOXML has two whole-shape animation targets (`spid=5`), no `p:pRg` or `p:charRg`, and the converter warns that the partial effect was applied to the whole paragraph. Exact inline animation targeting **fails**. [Stage4](HTML_PPTX_STAGE4_INLINE_STOP.md) tested a disposable character-range candidate, but generic offsets and clean visible WPS behavior remain unproven; Stage5 correctly skipped product implementation.

For actual WPS motion, timing and transitions, this stage made one bounded, read-only primary-screen capture feasibility attempt. `System.Drawing.Graphics.CopyFromScreen` returned `invalid handle` and wrote no image; the [probe record](../artifacts/html-pptx-stage6-final-20261122/desktop_capture_probe.json) records the error. No slideshow was driven, no pre-existing error dialog was touched, and no clean frame was obtained. Stage2's earlier COM/page-advance observations and polluted/black captures remain historical. Actual visible playback is **unverified**, not passed.

**Decision: STOP full high-fidelity acceptance; retain `best-effort`.** Static and package-structure gates pass; the video-object and precise inline-target gates fail; clean animation playback remains unverified. CI and targeted tests govern whether this evidence PR can merge, not whether the product has passed those device gates. Reacceptance needs lawful source media, a general inline offset mapping with precise WPS visible behavior, and unobstructed screen evidence for effects, triggers, delay, duration and transitions.
